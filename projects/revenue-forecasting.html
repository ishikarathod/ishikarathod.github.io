Revenue Forecasting

SQL and Python pipelines across 940K+ records, and the time series work that found a 12.7% revenue growth opportunity.

  • WhereShipDelight Logistics Technologies, Mumbai
  • WhenJan 2025 to May 2025
  • TypeTime Series
940K+records automated
18%efficiency gain
12.7%growth opportunity
Detail I/R®

Context

Analytics at a logistics company lives or dies on throughput. The reporting workflow was manual, which meant that by the time a number reached a decision maker it described a week that had already ended.

What I built

Automated SQL and Python data pipelines running across 940K+ records, replacing the manual assembly step and streamlining the analytics workflows that sat on top of it.

The forecast

With reliable inputs, time series forecasting models became worth building. They quantified a 12.7% revenue growth opportunity, which I worked through with supply chain and business teams to turn into strategic recommendations rather than a chart.

Outcome

An 18% operational efficiency gain, and results synthesised for senior leadership in a form they could act on.
